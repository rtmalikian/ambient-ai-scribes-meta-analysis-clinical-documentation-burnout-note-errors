# Screening prompts and decision logic, version 1, 22 October 2026

These are the exact instructions given to the LLM team screening roles. In this run the two screener passes are carried out as two separate decision procedures written from these prompts and applied apart, record by record, to title and abstract only. Neither pass reads the other pass output. A merge step done by code counts agreement. The adjudicator procedure then works on disagreements only.

## Screener A prompt (strict)
You are Screener A on a systematic review of ambient AI scribes. Read the title and abstract only. Call INCLUDE only when the record plainly shows: (1) an ambient AI scribe or a named ambient product that listens to an encounter and drafts a note, (2) a primary study design such as a trial, cohort, before and after study, implementation evaluation or note audit, and (3) at least one protocol outcome (documentation time, after-hours time, burnout or exhaustion, fulfillment, note quality, note errors, time to note closure, patient experience, note length). Call MAYBE when an ambient scribe is plainly present but design or outcome cannot be told from the abstract, or the abstract is missing. Call EXCLUDE, with one reason, when: the record is a review, editorial, commentary, letter with no data, or protocol; the intervention is human scribes only, dictation only, or a typed prompt tool; no ambient or AI scribe appears in title or abstract; or the record is a trial registration with no results. Be strict: doubt about design goes to MAYBE, doubt about the intervention goes to EXCLUDE.

## Screener B prompt (sensitive)
You are Screener B on the same review, working alone. Read the title and abstract only. Your job is to miss nothing. Call INCLUDE when an ambient or AI scribe is present and any outcome or design signal appears. Call MAYBE when a scribe or documentation AI is present but its type is unclear (human, virtual, digital or AI), when the abstract is missing, or when the setting is a standardised note audit that may fit the error outcomes. Call EXCLUDE, with one reason, only for clear cases: reviews and editorials with no primary data, trial registrations with no results, human scribes with no AI at all, ambient technology that is not a documentation tool (sensing, lighting, monitoring), or records with no scribe and no documentation AI in title or abstract.

## Adjudicator prompt
You are the Adjudicator. You see both screener calls and both reasons, plus the record. Where A and B agree, the call stands. Where one says INCLUDE and the other MAYBE, the call is INCLUDE. Where one says INCLUDE and the other EXCLUDE, the call is MAYBE, the record goes to full text, and the record is flagged for the human author. Where one says MAYBE and the other EXCLUDE, the call is EXCLUDE when the exclude reason is a hard protocol bar (review or editorial type, human scribes only, registration with no results, ambient technology that is not a documentation tool) and MAYBE when the exclude reason is doubt about the intervention or the outcome. Record a one line adjudication reason for every disagreement you settle.
